• The panel renders role="dialog" and aria-modal="true" automatically.
  • .kui-drawer-title is wired as aria-labelledby automatically when present in your content. Always include one.
  • Focus is trapped inside the drawer while open and returns to the opener on close.
  • Escape closes by default unless closeOnEscape: false; backdrop click closes by default unless closeOnBackdropClick: false. Disable both for required actions.
  • Backdrop dismissal only applies when the pointer interaction starts on the backdrop; selecting text or dragging from inside the drawer cannot close it accidentally.
  • The panel renders .kui-drawer-close itself, top-right of the header, when closable is true (the default); the header reserves space for it automatically. drawerContext.closable is retained for informational use only.
  • The imports are KuiButton and the rest of the renamed classes; ng update @kikita-labs/ui renames them. KuiDrawerContext, KUI_DRAWER_CONTEXT and kuiDrawer() keep their names.
  • Defaults come from defaults.drawer and are read each time a drawer opens; the close icon takes precedence over defaults.icons.close.
  • The backdrop and layers follow the new scrim and layer tokens, the close button reserves its space for titles rendered through a component portal, and the accessible name comes from the drawer messages.
