onIndexChange fires once on open with the clamped index and again on every navigation while the lightbox stays open. Photos only: video is out of scope.

  • Dragging a zoomed-in photo pans it. Panning stops when the pointer leaves the lightbox and always ends cleanly on pointer up or cancel, even outside the window (setPointerCapture).
  • The photo is not natively draggable or selectable, so a pan never becomes the browser's drag-image or text-selection gesture.
  • Mouse wheel and a trackpad pinch (a wheel event with ctrlKey) zoom by one zoomStep per event; scrolling up zooms in.
  • A touchscreen two-finger pinch zooms continuously, scaling from where the pinch began.
  • The panel is role="dialog" with aria-modal="true", labelled by a visually hidden heading such as Photo viewer, photo 2 of 4, or just the ariaLabel for a single photo.
  • The focus trap, focus restore to the trigger on close and the page scroll lock come from KuiDialog.
  • The counter is aria-live="polite" and is not rendered for a single photo.
  • Close, Prev, Next, Zoom in and Zoom out are icon buttons that get the native disabled at their bounds; Prev and Next are not rendered for a single photo.
  • Every thumbnail is a real button named Go to photo N of total with aria-current="true" on the viewed one.
  • Escape closes; ArrowLeft and ArrowRight go to the previous and next photo without wrapping; Home and End jump to the first and last; Tab cycles inside the lightbox.
  • Video.
  • A selectable prop: the grid and per-tile selection are your own composition.
  • A pan clamp aware of the natural image size: panning uses a fixed offset budget per zoom step.
  • Gallery grouping across several kuiMediaViewer() calls; each open is independent.
